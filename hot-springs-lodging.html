Three hot springs, one riverside base

The same volcanic geology that shaped the McKenzie heats the groundwater into some of Oregon's best-loved hot springs. Vida View Retreat sits on the river a few miles west of them, so every soak is an easy day trip.

20–25min

Belknap Hot Springs

Developed mineral pools and famous gardens on the McKenzie's bank near McKenzie Bridge. Easy, family-friendly soaking; day passes are sold, so check seasonal availability.

~45min

Terwilliger (Cougar) Hot Springs

Natural rock pools stepping down an old-growth ravine near Cougar Reservoir, a quarter-mile walk from the road. First-come day use with a fee; go early on summer weekends.

35–40min

Bigelow (Deer Creek) Hot Springs

A single fern-draped pool in a rock grotto right at the river's edge off the upper highway. Small, primitive, lukewarm, and a fine detour on a Blue Pool day.

Where to stay between soaks

Two private, gated retreats on the McKenzie River. Book one, or take the whole riverbank for a group.

The Bed & Breakfast

Sauna, heated pool & hot tub

Four designer suites elevated above the river, with a heated pool, Finnish sauna, and hot tub on site. The suites rent individually, which suits a couple's hot springs weekend. Ask about renting the whole house (3-night minimum).

The River Front House

A hot tub steps from the river

Three bedrooms at the water's edge on 750 feet of private frontage, with a riverside hot tub and fire pit. Three-night stays include paddleboards and inner tubes.

Hot springs questions

Good to know: there are no natural hot springs on our property. What you'll find is a Finnish sauna, a heated pool, and riverside hot tubs, the perfect way to end a day at the springs.
Where should I stay near Terwilliger Hot Springs?

Vida View Retreat in Vida, Oregon is roughly 45 minutes from Terwilliger (Cougar) Hot Springs and about 20–25 minutes from Belknap, directly on the McKenzie River. Choose the four-suite Bed & Breakfast with its heated pool and Finnish sauna, or The River Front House with a hot tub steps from the river.

How far is Vida View Retreat from Belknap Hot Springs?

About 20–25 minutes east on Highway 126, near McKenzie Bridge.

Does Vida View Retreat have its own hot springs?

No, there are no natural hot springs on the property. Guests have a Finnish sauna and heated pool at the Bed & Breakfast and riverside hot tubs, which make a perfect end to a day at the springs.

Do I need a reservation for the hot springs?

Belknap sells day passes for its pools; availability varies by season, so check ahead. Terwilliger is a first-come, first-served day-use site with a fee, so arrive early on summer weekends.

Is Terwilliger Hot Springs clothing optional?

Yes, by long-standing tradition. It's respectful in practice, but worth knowing before you go.
